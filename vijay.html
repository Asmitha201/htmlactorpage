vijay

oseph Vijay Chandrasekhar, known mononymously as Vijay, is a prominent Indian actor, dancer, playback singer, and philanthropist, primarily working in Tamil cinema. Early Life and Background Birth: Vijay was born on June 22, 1974, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India. He is the son of film director S. A. Chandrasekhar and playback singer Shoba Chandrasekhar. He had a sister named Vidhya, who passed away at a young age.
